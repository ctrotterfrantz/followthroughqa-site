The gap

The part nobody does well.

You're running audits. Your inspectors are catching what they see on the floor. But the patterns across accounts, the misses, the trends — that work happens in someone's head at 9pm Sunday, or it doesn't happen at all.

Followthrough is the layer that runs after. Same audits, same inspectors, same software you already use. New conversation with your customer.

What it does

Three things, done well.

01 / Patterns

The misses that keep happening.

Cross-account analysis surfaces the questions that fail most, the sites that trend down, and the inspectors whose scores don't match the floor. Evidence-backed, not vibes.

02 / Digests

The Monday morning brief.

Every Monday, every account gets a short, owner-readable summary. What needed attention last week. What's trending. What to bring up on the next call — with the audit IDs already pulled.

03 / QBR packets

The quarterly conversation, already built.

Branded, account-specific QBR materials — scorecards, trend charts, the misses you closed, the ones still open. Built from your audits, ready to walk into the room with.
